**The short version**

- I use analytics to see which pages get read. Only Google Analytics sets cookies.
- If you write to me through the contact form, your message reaches me in Slack. I use it to reply to you.
- There are no ads and no accounts on this site.
- Ask, and I’ll delete anything you’ve sent me.

The details are below, in plain language. Questions go through the contact page.

## Who runs this site

adithyabhat.com is my personal website. I’m Adithya NR, a product engineer based in Abu Dhabi. Wherever this page says “I” or “me”, it means me.

## What I collect

Two kinds of information:

- **What you send me.** If you use the contact form, I get your name, your email address and your message. The form also has a hidden field that helps catch spam bots; people never see it.
- **How the site gets used.** Analytics tools record things like which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (country or city), the device and browser, and which site linked here. I use this to see what people read and to keep the site fast, not to work out who you are.

## The services involved

A few services do the work behind the scenes:

- **Google Analytics** counts visits and page views. It sets cookies.
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** counts page views without cookies.
- **Vercel** hosts the site. Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights measure page views and loading speed, without cookies.
- **Slack** is where contact form messages arrive.
- **DatoCMS** stores and serves the writing and photos on this site.

Each one has its own privacy policy, linked above. I don’t control how they handle data on their side.

## Cookies and local storage

Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and similar) so it can tell a new visit from a returning one. The theme and sound switches remember your choice in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device. You can block or clear both in your browser settings, and the site works the same without them.

## How long I keep things

I keep what you send me until whichever comes first:

- you ask me to delete it,
- I stop using the service that stores it, or
- it’s no longer worth keeping.

Analytics data is kept for as long as each service’s retention settings allow. [Check: Google Analytics retention period]

## Your choices

You can ask what I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Send a note through the [contact page](/contact) and I’ll sort it out.

You can also block analytics with your browser’s tracking protection or a content blocker. Everything on the site still works.
