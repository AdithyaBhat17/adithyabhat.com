# Credits

## Sounds

The engine recording in `public/sounds/engine/` is trimmed and re-encoded
(Opus/WebM with an AAC/M4A fallback) from a public-domain recording by
BigSoundBank (Joseph Sardin), released under CC0 1.0. No attribution is
required; it's noted here as a courtesy.

- BigSoundBank sound #281: https://bigsoundbank.com/sound-0281.html

Every other sound on the site is synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio
API (`src/scripts/sound.ts`).
